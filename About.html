Section 01 • The Genesis

From Himalayan High Passes to Global Horizons

STACKLY began with a simple belief: the world’s most profound transformations happen when we leave our comfort zones. Founded in 2010 by high-altitude mountaineers, swiftwater kayakers, and outdoor educators, we saw that commercial tourism had become passive and predictable.

We set out to engineer expeditions that combine authentic adrenaline with certified leadership, real-time satellite safety telemetry, environmental conservation, and deep cultural immersion.

Why Travelers Trust Us

Four Pillars of STACKLY Expeditions

Engineered from over a decade of high-altitude logistics and international rescue standards.

Certified Leadership

Every lead guide is IMF, IRF, or PADI certified with a minimum of 8 years field experience and Wilderness First Responder (WFR) training.

Satellite Telemetry

Real-time GPS satellite beacons, continuous pulse oximetry tracking, and 24/7 dedicated high-altitude helicopter evacuation insurance backup.

Small Group Dynamic

We cap expedition groups at 12 travelers, ensuring personalized guide attention, minimal trail footprint, and authentic team camaraderie.

Local Empowerment

Over 85% of our trail crew, porters, and cooks are hired from indigenous Himalayan valleys with fair living wages and high-altitude gear.

Section 05

Five Specialized Adventure Disciplines

We operate across five comprehensive outdoor sectors, managed by specialized discipline directors:

  • Alpine Trekking: High altitude ridge hikes, snowy pass crossings, and technical summit ascents up to 6,000m.
  • Whitewater Rapids: Grade IV & V river rafting expeditions, cliff jumping, and rapid navigation.
  • Aerial Paragliding: Tandem cross-country thermal flights with veteran FAI-certified pilots.
  • Oceanic Scuba Diving: Certified PADI exploration diving along pristine coral reefs and shipwrecks.
  • Wilderness Camping: Geodesic all-weather domes, starlight wilderness camps, and Leave No Trace bivouacs.

Section 06 • Sustainability

Responsible Travel & Environmental Stewardship

Our commitment to ecological preservation is built into every expedition budget. We adhere strictly to international Leave No Trace (LNT) guidelines:

Zero Single-Use Plastic: Every traveler receives reusable aluminum water canisters and high-grade filtration pouches on trail.
4,500+ kg Trail Waste Retrieved: On every descent, our guides and porters collect non-biodegradable waste left behind by past visitors.
Direct Community Investment: 10% of expedition net proceeds fund solar heaters and education supplies in remote Himalayan villages.
