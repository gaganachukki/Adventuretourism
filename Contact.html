Section 07 • FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your STACKLY expedition.

What fitness level is required for high altitude treks?

For moderate treks (up to 4,200m), being able to jog 5km in 35-40 minutes is recommended. For challenging treks like Ladakh or winter passes, cardiovascular endurance, stair climbing with a weighted pack, and lung capacity conditioning are crucial. We provide a 4-week training blueprint upon booking.

What safety gear and medical provisions are included?

Every trek group carries certified portable oxygen cylinders, pulse oximeters for morning and evening vitals checking, hyperbaric safety chambers where required, and comprehensive mountaineering first-aid kits. All guides hold Wilderness First Responder (WFR) certification.

What is your cancellation and rescheduling policy?

We offer 100% trip credit or date transfers if notified at least 15 days prior to departure. If adverse weather or governmental closures affect the trail, we arrange alternative safe routes or provide full voucher redemption valid for 18 months.

Can solo travelers join group expeditions?

Absolutely! Over 45% of our explorers join solo. We pair solo travelers of the same gender in twin-sharing dome tents, or you can opt for private single tent occupancy at a nominal supplement.
